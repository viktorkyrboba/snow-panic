Snow Panic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Developer: the Snow Panic developer, Republic of Moldova · Contact: snowpanicgame@gmail.com

Snow Panic is a casual downhill game. We collect as little data as possible. This policy applies to all versions of the game: the website, CrazyGames and the Android app.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, coins, rides, settings, friends list and best runs are saved in your browser or app storage. They stay on your device unless you use the online features below.

Online features (optional)

Online races, lobbies, friends and crew boards only work after you choose a nickname and agree to the rules. Then we process on our server (Supabase):

  • your nickname (please do not use your real full name);
  • a random device id created by the game (not an advertising id and not linked to your identity);
  • your scores on crew boards and the crew code you entered;
  • during a race or lobby: your position on the track, ride and ready status, sent live to the other players in that race or lobby;
  • while the game is open: your online status (nickname, ride, whether you are in the menu, a lobby or playing), visible to players who added you as a friend;
  • reports about nicknames (reason and the reporter's random id), used only for moderation.

There is no chat and no other free text besides the nickname. Live race and lobby data is not stored after the session ends. You can delete your crew data any time in the game (Crew → Delete my crew data). Data of inactive players is deleted after 12 months.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game creates a picture and a link with your nickname, run time and route. It goes only where you choose to share it.

Ads

On game platforms (for example CrazyGames), any ads are shown by the platform under its own privacy policy. If the Android app shows ads, they are provided by Google AdMob under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell your data.

Purchases

In the Android app, purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but online features are intended for players aged 13 and older. Nicknames are filtered and can be reported, and there is no chat.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by writing to snowpanicgame@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
